🗞️ THE HOBBY DESK — WHAT'S MOVING THE MARKET

*The week across the hobby. Sourced from public reporting — every item links to verify.*

  • Topps Definitive Basketball made its NBA debut on Sept 17 at $7,499.99 a box: eight cards, six of them autographs on average, handed out by EQL raffle rather than sold over a counter. That is roughly $937 a card before the wrapper comes off, with Cooper Flagg, Dylan Harper and Kon Knueppel leading the rookie class and a Stephen Curry one-of-one on the checklist. Worth saying plainly — nothing about this product is built for someone who buys one card at a time, and that is fine. The reason to care is that the autographs coming out of boxes like this are what set the top-of-market numbers every other price gets measured against for the rest of the season. — per Sports Illustrated
