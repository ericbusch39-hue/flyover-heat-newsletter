🗞️ THE HOBBY DESK — WHAT'S MOVING THE MARKET

*The week across the hobby. Sourced from public reporting — every item links to verify.*

  • PSA opens a new Standard tier on Sept 14: $59.99 a card, 90 to 100 business days, $1,000 max declared value — and it queues behind the Value and Bulk orders already in the building. The Value levels most collectors actually used, $24.99 Bulk through $64.99 Max, stay paused until the backlog falls from 9.9 million cards to 5 million, so the cheapest door open right now costs more than three of the four tiers it isn't replacing. Express also went $149 to $199. Before you box up a stack: at $59.99 and a four-to-five-month wait, the fee and the calendar are most of the argument on anything but a genuinely valuable card. — per Sports Illustrated
